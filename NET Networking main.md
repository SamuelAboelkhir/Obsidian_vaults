## Wireless networking (802.11)
- Managed by the institute of electrical and electronics engineers (IEEE) standards committee
### Wi-Fi
- Devices marked with the Wi-Fi trademark are tested by the Wi-Fi alliance, which test for interoperability
![[Pasted image 20260711233102.png]]
- The first 3 standards are no longer in use
### 4G and LTE
- Long Term Evolution (LTE) or 4G is used wireless providers for mobile devices
- Supports download rates of 150 Mb/s
- LTE Advanced (LTE-A) supports 300 Mb/s
### 5G
- Aims to reach 10 Gb/s, but more reasonably ranges between 100-900 Mb/s
- 5G significantly enhanced the capabilities of IoT devices
### Satellite networking
- A non-terrestrial form of communication
- Reasonable in speed at around 100 Mb/s download and 5 Mb/s upload, yet still slower than terrestrial alternatives
- Much higher in cost
- Normally used in remote sites
- Has relatively high latency of 250 ms both up and down, although newer tech is reducing this significantly
- Requires direct line of sight from the receiver to the satellite
