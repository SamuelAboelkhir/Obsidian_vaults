- **Atomic mass** → one isotope, one atom.
- **Atomic weight** → weighted average of naturally occurring isotopes.
- **Molecular mass** → sum of atomic masses in one molecule.
- **Molar mass** → grams per mole of a substance.
- **Mole** → a counting unit equal to exactly **6.02214076 × 10²³** specified entities (atoms, molecules, ions, etc.).
