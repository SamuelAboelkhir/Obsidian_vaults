# Lipodystrophy: (unit 2.5.6)

An abnormal decrease in body fat. This condition can be the result of genetic inheritance like familial partial lipodystrophy or it can be acquired lipodystrophy which can be the result of an autoimmune reaction or drug treatment.

# Neutral lipid storage disease: (2-3 unit 2.5.7)

A rare genetic disease that results in the body’s inability to properly store fat. It’s a monogenic disease meaning that it’s the result of a defect in a single gene. It has two forms, **NLSD-M** which is characterized by a type of heart disorder called **Cardiomyopathy.** The other type **NLSD-I** is characterized by **Ichthyosis** where the skin of the patient is dry and rough and takes a fish scale-like appearance.

# Metabolic Syndrome:

**Metabolic syndrome** is a polygenic disease associated with mutations affecting a group of non-allelic genes. It’s characterized by a cluster of pathologies, including high blood levels of triacylglycerol, cholesterol, and LDL, hyperglycemia, and insulin resistance.
