# Lipolysis: (unit 2.5.3)

1- ATGL (Adipose triglyceride lipase): Catalysis the first step converting triacylglycerol into diacylglycerol.

2- HSL (Hormone-sensitive lipase): Catalysis the 2nd step where diacylglycerol is converted into monoacylglycerol.

3- MGL (Monoacylglyceride lipase): Catalysis the 3rd step where monoacylglycerol is converted into the last molecule of fatty acid and a molecule of glycerol.

4- Catecholamines: A family of hormones including adrenaline and noradrenaline that binds to a receptor at the membrane of adipocytes to cause an increase in the concentration of cAMP.

5- PKA: Protein kinase A, it phosphorylates perilipin to 1) activate ABDH5 and 2) cause a remodeling that increases the access of enzymes to the lipid droplet, and HSL to activate it.

6- FABP4 (Fatty acid-binding protein 4): A chaperon that protects and transports fatty acids through the cytoplasm to the cell membrane.

7- ABHD5 (abhydrolase domain containing 5): When activated can recruit ATGL to the surface of lipid droplets to begin the first step of lipolysis.
