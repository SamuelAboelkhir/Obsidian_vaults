# Hypnagogia
- This is the transitional state from wakefulness, to sleep
- It's the state preceding sleep, where you're drifting into sleep, but still haven't lost consciousness
## Hypnagogic hallucinations
- hypnagogic imagery / hypnagogic hallucinations are images experienced during the transition
- The brain starts producing some of the characteristics of sleep during that time:
	- visual scenes or images appear spontaneously
	- thoughts become less logically controlled
	- one thought can morph into another without you deliberately making the connection
	- you may see people, places, objects, movement, etc.
	- the imagery can become surprisingly vivid
	- you can still have some awareness that you're lying in bed
	- you can sometimes deliberately _nudge_ the direction of your thoughts
	- afterward, you may remember fragments, or essentially nothing
## Hypnagogic jerk
- This is that super annoying phenomena where you experience an involuntary movement while falling asleep
- When accompanied by hypnagogic imagery it can be experienced alongside sensations such as falling from a high place or touching something extremely hot
## Lucid dreaming
- I've been able to experience it before
- A possible progression into it is something like this:
	- **Normal thought:** "I'm going to imagine walking through a forest."
	- **Looser thought:** "I'm walking through a forest... there's a house..."
	- **Spontaneous imagery:** The forest starts appearing without you deliberately visualizing it.
	- **Narrative autonomy:** Things start happening that you didn't decide should happen.
	- **Increasing immersion:** The imagery becomes spatial, vivid, and occasionally feels almost perceptual.
	- **Loss of waking sensory dominance:** Your actual bedroom becomes less salient.
	- **Dream:** You're effectively _in_ the scene.
	- **If awareness survives:** "Oh shit, I'm dreaming." → **lucid dream.**
## Sleep paralysis
- A phenomena triggerd by REM atonia (the inhibition of skeletal muscle during sleep) persisting into the waking state
- It can be experienced both while still falling asleep (hypnagogia) or waking up (hypnopompia)
- One of the most uncomfortable things one can experience if awareness returns before skeletal muscles control is back, as you're essentially paralyzed, trying to move but completely unable to
- I managed to experience it with hypnagogic imagery still going on... not very pleasant at all, as I was literally seeing things around my room that didn't exist, while not being able to move
- Fun fact, I actually intentionally withheld myself from letting my imagination go any wilder at the time so that I wouldn't end up living a real life horror movie
## A super cool experience
- During sleep paralysis, subjective time can feel distorted, potentially producing some very strange experiences
- Managing to maintain awareness at the time, lets you experience that distortion
- For me, I managed to experience sunrise a couple of times in that state, seeing the sun rise up very quickly
- This was possibly a mix of 
	- Transient awareness giving me an external reference to the position of the sun, rather than maintained visual observation
	- Hypnagogia filling in the blanks
	- Sleep paralysis giving me the distorted time perception
